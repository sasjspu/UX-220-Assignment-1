## Research
The article "Using e-portfolio for learning goal setting to facilitate self-regulated learning of high school students" highlights the importance of goal setting in academic environments, especially for self-regulated learning, in which "learning goals act as facilitators for students' efforts and improvements" (Chang et al. 1240). The same principle can consequently be applied to my personal goals. By setting goals and following through with related activities, including reflection and self-assessment, the goals I have outlined for myself will help facilitate my efforts for the future.

## Works Cited
Chang, Chi-Cheng, et al. “Using E-Portfolio for Learning Goal Setting to Facilitate Self-Regulated Learning of High School Students.” Behaviour & Information Technology, vol. 37, no. 12, Dec. 2018, pp. 1237–51. EBSCOhost, https://doi.org/10.1080/0144929X.2018.1496275.
